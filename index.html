Who We Are

A studio, not
a showroom aisle.

Heaven Furniture Mart is Chattogram's home for bespoke furniture and interior styling — living, bedroom, dining and office pieces designed around your room, your wood, and your taste, then hand-built in our own workshop.

Since 2020, hundreds of homeowners have walked into our Agrabad showroom with an idea and walked out with furniture that fits their space exactly. That's the whole practice, in three words: Designed. Crafted. Customized.
